# Marketing pack - Scout Explores Tennessee (louda, reduced scope)

**Scope note (read first):** I scoped this run to **listing copy, hooks, captions and Reel concepts**, as agreed in the ColorBrain brief. I did **not** run the competitor/viral-content research (no live platform data was reachable in this sandbox), so there is **no** viral-content table and **no** claim here about what is trending. Nothing below cites a sale, review, award or ranking. Every product claim matches the finished file. The character-video prompts (Nano Banana character + 10 Flow clips) were **not** produced: the book's own character, Scout, is a drawn fox, so Reels use the book's pages and cover as footage. Say if you want the full character/Flow package added.

## 1. Niche
- **Category:** children's state-themed coloring and fact book (print).
- **Niche:** Tennessee gift and travel coloring book for ages 6-9 with a recurring character.
- **Key buyer:** a parent or grandparent in or visiting Tennessee (also teachers/homeschoolers doing a state-studies unit); the user is a 6-9-year-old.
- **Positioning angle:** one place per page, a fun fact per page, and a hunt for three hidden flag stars - "color it, learn it, find it".

## 2. What the finished book actually contains (claims you may make)
- 56 printed pages, A4; 24 single-sided coloring pages with a blank back behind each.
- 20 Tennessee cities and towns across West, Middle and East Tennessee, plus a Welcome page, a Tennessee Flag page, a "My Family's Town" drawing page and a Goodbye page.
- A short fun fact on every page; Scout the explorer fox on every page.
- **3 hidden flag stars on every coloring page** (the Flag page's stars are the flag's own).
- Travel checklist with page numbers, color test page, "This book belongs to" page.
- Towns: Memphis, Tiptonville, Paris, Jackson, Clarksville, Nashville, Franklin, Columbia, Murfreesboro, Cookeville, Chattanooga, Sweetwater, Oak Ridge, Knoxville, Sevierville, Pigeon Forge, Gatlinburg, Johnson City, Kingsport, Bristol.
- Not claimed: age-tested, educator-approved, "bestselling", paper weight, or any specific paper stock.

## 5. Three Reel concepts (original structures; use real page footage)
Footage needed for all three: screen-recorded flips of the PDF, phone video of a page being colored, close-ups of the cover. Do not film a child's face.

**Reel A - "Find the stars" (hook 1 / challenge format), 15-20 s**
- 0-3 s: close-up of a Memphis page; text "3 stars hidden - find them!"
- 3-12 s: finger points to each badge; each fills with color as a time-lapse
- 12-17 s: flip through 4 more towns quickly
- 17-20 s: cover + "Scout Explores Tennessee" CTA
- On-screen text: "Hidden star 1 / 2 / 3"
- Caption: "How fast can you spot all three? Scout hid them on every page. 🦊⭐ #TennesseeKids #ColoringBook"
- CTA: "Link in bio to get the book."

**Reel B - "One page, one fun fact" (hook 7 / tutorial-reveal), 20-25 s**
- 0-4 s: hands color the Knoxville Sunsphere; text "Today's town: Knoxville"
- 4-14 s: time-lapse coloring
- 14-20 s: finished page + the fun fact appears as text
- 20-25 s: "20 towns inside" + cover
- Caption: "A gold glass ball 266 feet tall! Which Tennessee town should we color next?"
- CTA: "Save this for your next road trip."

**Reel C - "Screen-free road trip" (hook 6 / POV), 15-20 s**
- 0-3 s: POV, hands holding the book in a car seat; text "Long drive? Color the town you're visiting."
- 3-12 s: three quick cuts (Gatlinburg, Chattanooga, Nashville pages) with crayons in the shot
- 12-17 s: travel checklist page being ticked
- 17-20 s: cover
- Caption: "Tick off each town as you go. Perfect for Tennessee road trips."
- CTA: "Grab a copy - link in bio."
