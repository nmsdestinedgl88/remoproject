# Re-render prompts - Scout Explores Tennessee (round 2)

Round 1 gave 20 of 24 pages (sheets 2-6; sheet 1 was never uploaded). Ten of those failed the star count or had a wrong landmark, so these four sheets re-render **15 pages**: the 4 missing from sheet 1 plus the 11 that failed (`page_review.md`). Attach the Scout reference images from `reference/` to every sheet. Save as the filenames shown.

**What changed in the prompts:** no frames or ruled lines around pages or the header zone; no solid black fills; Scout exactly once; a hard three-star rule with numbered positions and a ban on decorative stars; Chattanooga is now a funicular on rails (not a gondola).

- **Top-right:** The Tennessee Flag

```
Create one sheet holding four separate coloring book pages in a 2 x 2 grid. The sheet is A4 portrait
proportions (1:1.414), at the highest resolution available (4K). Leave a plain white gutter between the four
pages and around the outside, with no dividing lines or frames, and let no artwork cross a gutter. STYLE
(identical for every page): pure black line art on a pure white background. No grey, no shading, no gradients,
no color fills. One consistent bold outline weight (about 4 pt at A4), slightly thicker on outer contours and
thinner on interior detail. Friendly, detailed children's-activity-book cartoon style for ages 6-9: rich but
readable backgrounds, no tiny fiddly areas, every shape fully closed so it can be colored in. NO text,
letters, numbers, signatures, watermarks anywhere in the artwork (signs and banners stay blank and unmarked).
NO borders, frames or boxes around any page or around the empty top zone, and no ruled lines across the page.
NO large solid-black filled areas: shadows, water and cave voids are drawn with outlines and thin ripple lines
only, so everything can be colored. No copyrighted characters, logos or brands. Leave the top 18% of every
page completely empty white (a title and fun fact are added there later). Keep all artwork inside a 6% safe
margin of each page. SCOUT (match the attached reference image exactly - same face, proportions and outfit on
every page): a friendly young fox explorer with big eyes, pointed ears, fluffy cheek fur, a wide-brimmed
safari hat with a band, a small backpack with a round compass on it, a neckerchief tied at the neck, a vest
with two chest pockets over a short-sleeved shirt, shorts with a pocket, lace-up hiking boots, and a big bushy
tail with a zigzag white tip. Scout is about one third of the page height, the clear focal subject, and wears
the identical outfit on every page. Scout appears EXACTLY ONCE on each page - never two foxes, never a twin or
reflection. STARS: hide exactly THREE small five-pointed stars (Tennessee flag stars) on each page, no more
and no fewer. Each star sits inside its own small plain circle, like a round badge about 3% of the page width,
outlined and empty inside so it can be colored. Place them exactly where the page brief says and number them
1, 2, 3. Do NOT draw any other star anywhere: no star patterns, star decorations, star medals or star-shaped
sparkles on guitars, saddles, costumes, signs or the sky. Before finishing each page, count the star badges:
there must be exactly three. Top-left page - Welcome to Tennessee!: Scout waves hello beside a big blank road
sign at a state welcome area; a guitar leans on the sign post; rolling blue-ridge mountains, pine trees and
fluffy clouds behind. Star badges: 1: on the blank sign's corner; 2: in the clouds at upper left; 3: on a rock
at lower right. Top-right page - The Tennessee Flag: Scout stands proudly at lower left pointing up at a large
rectangular flag on a tall pole filling the upper two thirds of the page. The flag shows a large circle at
left-center with a thin ring around it, containing three five-pointed stars arranged in a triangle, plus a
narrow vertical stripe at the fly end. The three stars inside the circle ARE the three flag stars for this
page. Star badges: 1, 2, 3: the three stars inside the flag's circle (each star in the flag is a plain
outlined star; the circle is the flag's own circle) - draw no other stars on the page. Bottom-left page -
Memphis: Scout waves on a riverbank; behind, a huge glass pyramid building and a riverboat on the wide
Mississippi River; in the foreground three ducks march in a line toward a small fountain; a blues guitar rests
on a bench. Star badges: 1: on the pyramid's peak; 2: on the riverboat's smokestack; 3: on the fountain.
Bottom-right page - Tiptonville: Scout waves from a wooden dock on a calm lake dotted with tall bald cypress
trees standing in the water with knobby knees; a bald eagle soars overhead; ducks and lily pads on the water.
Star badges: 1: in the sky near the eagle; 2: on a cypress trunk; 3: on a lily pad. Detail level for all
pages: medium (ages 6-9). Keep each page's artwork inside its own safe margin and leave the top 18% of every
page empty. Output only the sheet itself, with no mockup, table, hands or pencils.
```
