Every number below traces to a search-snippet source named in the table, and anything not confirmed is marked **CHECK**. This is a strategic estimate, not financial advice or a sales forecast. Exchange rate used: **about ₦1,375 per US$** (mid-market reference seen on a rate site, date unconfirmed; sources showed ~₦1,360-1,376 - [Pluang](https://pluang.com/en/tools/currency-converter/usd-ngn), [Eversend](https://eversend.co/currency/usd-to-ngn)). Check a timestamped rate before using the naira figures.

## 1. Market & product
Print children's coloring/fact book, A4 (8.27 x 11.69 in), 56 printed pages (24 single-sided art pages), black-ink interior; buyers are US-based parents, grandparents and teachers (ages 6-9). Positioning: a Tennessee-specific, character-led book with a fun fact and a 3-star hunt on every page.

## 5. Recommendation
- **Recommended list price: $9.99 (≈ ₦13,736).**
- **Defensible range: $8.99 (≈ ₦12,361) to $11.99 (≈ ₦16,486).** Floor bounded by the Tennessee reader ($8.99) and the loose $5-$15 band; ceiling below Montpelier's $15.99 premium gift book, and a 56-page single-sided book has less content than a 100+ page value book.
- **Why $9.99:** third-party sources say KDP's 60% royalty starts at $9.99 and the rate is 50% below it ([Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-print-royalties/), [v13](https://v13.net/2025/05/amazon-kdp-just-cut-your-royalties/)) - **CHECK in KDP**; the cutoff differs by source ($9.98 vs $9.99). If that holds, $9.99 is the lowest price in the higher-royalty band.
- **Printing cost:** about **$2.84** for a large-trim (A4) black-ink book of 24-110 pages (sources: [kdpbuilder](https://kdpbuilder.com/resources/kdp-print-pricing-worked-examples), [Cambric](https://cambric.pub/guides/kdp-printing-cost-guide/); KDP's own help page [G201834340](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340)) - **CHECK in KDP's calculator; A4 may count as large trim.**
- **Royalty per copy = (rate x list price) - printing cost.** Amazon.com, estimate only:

| List price | ₦ | Royalty rate (CHECK) | Royalty/copy | ₦/copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $7.99 | ₦10,986 | 50% | $1.16 | ₦1,588 |
| $8.99 | ₦12,361 | 50% | $1.66 | ₦2,276 |
| $9.99 | ₦13,736 | 60% | $3.15 | ₦4,337 |
| $10.99 | ₦15,111 | 60% | $3.75 | ₦5,162 |
| $11.99 | ₦16,486 | 60% | $4.35 | ₦5,987 |
| $12.99 | ₦17,861 | 60% | $4.95 | ₦6,812 |

At $9.99: 0.60 x 9.99 - 2.84 = **$3.15** (≈ ₦4,337). Dropping to $8.99 cuts it to $1.66 - not worth it unless you want a lower shelf price.
- **Platform notes:** one global US-dollar price. Nigeria-based royalty payment method/currency: the ColorBrain skill notes say KDP pays Nigeria by wire in NGN - **CHECK** KDP's payment help page.
- **Risk flags:** no Amazon price or sales evidence for this niche; printing cost and royalty cutoff unverified; naira rate date unconfirmed; Gallopade's $3.95 shows low-price competition exists; the AI-generated art disclosure applies at upload.

## 6. Platform ranking (only on evidence gathered)
1. **Amazon KDP paperback - primary.** Print product, US family/gift buyers; KDP prints on demand with no upfront inventory. *Why not elsewhere:* the other platforms were not researched.
2. **Not researched / cannot rank:** Etsy printable PDF, Teachers Pay Teachers, Gumroad, Payhip, Lulu, IngramSpark, Shopify/direct. They may suit a printable-PDF version for homeschool buyers, but no evidence was gathered.
3. **Ruled out:** Apple Books (no coloring ebooks found, only apps; Stage 1), Selar (Nigerian-buyer marketplace; mismatch with a US/Tennessee audience - inferred, not researched).
