## 2. Script (142 words, about 58 seconds)
1. Half of Costco's operating profit comes from a membership fee, not from the shelves.
2. Odo, a made-up Lagos founder, copies the model with one warehouse.
3. He sells rice, oil and tomatoes close to cost. Members pay N30,000 a year to shop.
4. The fee is where the profit lives.

7. Costco members renew around nine times in ten, so the fee comes back every year.
8. Take Odo to 200,000 members and the fee alone is N6 billion.
9. Landlords and lenders want that renewal math on paper.
10. Manetrust Solutions writes the financial projections and pitch deck behind a membership business. DM us.

(Spoken in the voiceover as "Maintrust". On screen "Manetrust".)

**Beat map:** hook 1 / lock-in 2 / body 1 (gap, then mechanism) 3-4 / rehook 1 = 4 / body 2 (math) 5-6 / rehook 2 and scale 7-8 / closing bridge 9 (9 words, bottleneck = documentation of the unit economics) / CTA 10.
**Fact and math check:** 5,000 x N30,000 = N150m. N1.2m x 2.5% = N30,000 per member; x 5,000 = N150m. Total N300m, fees are exactly half. 200,000 x N30,000 = N6bn. Every label separates fee income from shelf profit, and none mixes revenue with profit. The Costco "half" is the fee against operating income.

## 9. Salesbrain notes
- **Model:** Storytelling (primary), Value Selling (support). The buyer needs to see the money in a figure they can check, and the checkable math is supplied by the fictional arithmetic.
- **Awareness level:** the buyer knows the problem (capital for growth) but not that lenders read documented renewal math. Medium length.
- **One idea:** a membership fee makes the profit predictable, and predictable profit has to be shown on paper to be funded.
- **Proof ledger:** *Supported:* Costco fee and operating income (reported); Costco renewal around 90% (secondary, confirm). *Seller's own facts:* the Manetrust service list (financial projections, pitch deck) and "DM us". *Fictional and labelled:* Odo and every Odo figure. *Needs proof, not used:* client counts, funds raised, turnaround times, guarantees. No risk-lowering line is included because no true one was supplied.
- **Recommended CTA:** line 10. **Alternate A:** "Manetrust Solutions builds the membership projections lenders ask for. DM us." **Alternate B:** "Planning a club of your own? Maintrust writes the plan and the deck. DM us."
- **Proof that would strengthen the piece:** one real Manetrust-written projection or funded client, if you can stand behind it.
- **Readiness: 7.5 / 10.** The math is checkable and the hinge is natural. The two things holding it back are the unconfirmed renewal figure and the lack of a Manetrust proof point.
