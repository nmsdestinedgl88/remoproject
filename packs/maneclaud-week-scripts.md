## Tuesday: Teaching (protagonist, Lemo) "Chike and the 50 days"

**Mechanism.** Cash conversion cycle = days inventory + days receivable − days payable ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_conversion_cycle)). Chike's numbers are story figures.

**Script**
1. Chike sells 400 cartons of provisions a month and still pays his supplier late.
2. The reason sits in one number: the cash conversion cycle.
3. Chike holds stock for 30 days. Shops pay him 40 days after delivery. His supplier wants payment in 20. Thirty plus forty minus twenty is 50 days. For 50 days, his cash sits somewhere else.
4. Watch what he changes.
5. He holds stock for 20 days and asks shops to pay in 25. Twenty plus twenty-five minus twenty is 25 days. His cash comes back 25 days sooner.
6. That cash pays for a second depot.
7. A lender wants to see this cycle in your projections.
8. A second depot needs a lender who can read his cycle.
