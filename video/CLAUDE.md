# Sevun promo videos — house rules

These apply to every edition in this folder (sevun, mechanics, tax, cyber, and any new one).

## Captions

- **Captions sit in the footer, never over the presenter's face.** Place caption pills / kinetic
  captions in the lower part of the frame, below the chin line (portrait 720×1280: top edge of
  the caption block around y 900–960, bottom edge no lower than y ~1130, so it stays above the
  platform UI band at the very bottom). The client flagged captions landing on the presenter's
  forehead in the cyber cut.
- Check every caption against the face in snapshots before rendering: no caption may cross the
  presenter's eyes, forehead or mouth in any shot.
- When a shot also has a graphic or widget (glass panels, calculator, cards), give the footer to
  the caption and move the widget up into the chest/mid-frame area, or put the widget beside the
  presenter. The caption owns the footer.
