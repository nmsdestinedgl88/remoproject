# 01 Direction (artistbrain pass, applied)

**Job in one sentence:** make a viewer feel that a mortgage company is really a cement factory's sales arm, and that the loop is airtight.

**Five angles:** Literal: finance diagrams. Human: a family at a locked door. Environmental: factory and city. Metaphorical: *a cement bag is the unit of account; every flow ends in a bag.* Oblique: the whole film is a ledger that gets *poured* into a city.
**Chosen:** metaphorical. Every scene resolves into a cement bag, and the ground itself changes from dull paper-grey (idle capacity) to charged ink-violet (machine running).

**POV name: "POURED LEDGER".** Matte cement-grey isometric solids on warm paper, ruled ledger lines, stencil capitals and a monospace data face. Heavy things settle once and stay. Purple is the only light source.

**Clichés avoided:** dark-navy neon fintech; pastel flat-icon corporate explainer; money-green + gold (previous video in this repo); stock hand-shake / coin-on-globe.

**Emotion map:** core = *tension that resolves into inevitability* (strong, steady -> restless in the middle -> still at the end). Levers: chroma muted in the problem (S1) and saturated only in purple/green as the model engages; value range widens to deep ink in the finale; shapes: blocky/rectilinear = trust (bags, houses, banks); diagonal/angular only for the problem (door lock, red bars, DEFAULT).
**Colour meaning by repetition:** purple = DHF (the engine), red = friction/default/25%, green = solved/lender paid/new owner. Used once per frame as the accent.
**Style:** flat-shaded isometric solids (3 face tones), 6 px ink outline on hero objects, no gradients on forms, soft purple halo only on DHF objects. Light grain over everything. Runner-up style: blueprint line-art on dark (rejected: reads as generic tech).
**Shape language:** 70% rectangles/cuboids, 30% circles (gauges, coins, loop). Silhouette hero: the cement bag (pillowed cuboid with a stencil band).
**Collision rule log:** designbrain wanted a monoline-on-dark family (more "code-built"); artistbrain intent (poured, material, paper->ink arc) kept, spec adjusted.

**Distinctiveness check:** first three looks a model reaches for: (1) dark navy + neon glow gradients, (2) pastel flat corporate icons, (3) dark green + gold. Chosen look differs on palette (light cement paper + ink + one purple), type (stencil Archivo 900 + Plex Mono, not Inter/Poppins) and motion (settle-once weight, stamp impacts, no float). PASS.
