# 90 Video Moods & Themes with Font Pairings
### All fonts are free (Google Fonts — SIL OFL or Apache licensed)

> **Note on classification:**
> - **Moods (01–40):** Emotional/aesthetic feel — *how* the video should feel.
> - **Themes (41–90):** Industry, purpose, or genre — *what* the video is about.
> These are independent axes. A video has both (e.g., a hospital training module = *Calm* mood + *Medical* theme).
