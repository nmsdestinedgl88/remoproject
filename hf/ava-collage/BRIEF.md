## Intent

60 s vertical infomercial for Ava Consultants & Urology Centre (Uyo): minimal-access prostate surgery for diabetic men and the family who help decide. Assured, human, exact. Look: Vox-style mixed-media paper collage (torn photo cutouts, marker annotations, flat colour fields, halftone), a different colour field per scene. Built with HyperFrames, motion-graphic's deterministic seek/critique rules and vox-explainer's collage vocabulary.
